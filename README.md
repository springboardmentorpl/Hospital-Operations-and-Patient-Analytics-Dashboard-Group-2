# Hospital Operations and Patient Analytics Dashboard(Group2)

## Dashboard Guide

The Hospital Operations and Patient Analytics Dashboard is an interactive healthcare analytics project developed using Tableau and Power BI. The dashboard suite helps hospital management analyze admissions, patient flow, department performance, revenue, and resource utilization.

The project contains four dashboards:

1. Hospital Overview
2. Patient Flow
3. Department Analysis
4. Resource Utilization

---

# Dashboard Navigation

The dashboards are connected through navigation buttons.

### Navigation Buttons

- Hospital Overview
- Patient Flow
- Department Analysis
- Resource Utilization

### Navigation Flow

Hospital Overview → Patient Flow → Department Analysis → Resource Utilization

The selected dashboard button is highlighted, while the remaining buttons are displayed in the normal style.

---

# 1. Hospital Overview Dashboard

## Purpose

The Hospital Overview Dashboard provides a high-level view of hospital performance and operations. It helps management understand admission trends, patient demographics, bed utilization, occupancy, revenue, and department efficiency.

## Filters

### Admission Month

Used to analyze hospital performance for a selected month or time period.

### Admission Type

Used to compare different admission categories and understand patient inflow.

### Department Name

Used to view admissions, revenue, and performance for a selected department.

### Ward Type

Used to compare admissions and resource usage across different ward types.

### Age Group

Used to analyze admissions and discharges across different patient age categories.

## KPI Cards

### Total Admissions

Shows the total number of patient admissions and helps measure hospital workload.

### Occupancy Rate

Shows the percentage of hospital beds occupied and helps monitor bed availability.

### Readmission Rate

Measures the percentage of patients admitted again and helps identify readmission patterns.

### Bed Utilization Rate

Shows how effectively hospital beds are being used.

### Department Efficiency

Helps compare the operational performance of different departments.

## Charts

### Monthly Admission Trend

A line chart is used to identify admission patterns and changes over time.

### Admissions by Admission Type

A bar chart is used to compare admission categories and understand patient inflow.

### Admissions by Ward Type

A donut or pie chart is used to analyze patient distribution across different ward types.

### Admissions by Age Group

A bar chart is used to understand which age groups contribute most to hospital admissions.

### Discharges by Age Group

A bar chart is used to compare discharged patients across different age groups.

### Total Revenue by Departments

A bar chart is used to compare revenue generated by each department and identify high-revenue departments.

### Average LOS by Department

A bar chart is used to compare the average length of patient stay across departments.

## Dashboard Insight

This dashboard helps management monitor overall hospital performance, identify admission trends, understand patient demographics, analyze revenue, and support resource planning.

# 2. Patient Flow Dashboard

## Purpose

The Patient Flow Dashboard focuses on patient movement and workflow throughout the hospital. It helps analyze admissions, discharges, transfers, age distribution, and length of stay.

## Filters

### Admission Date

Used to analyze patient flow for a selected date range.

### Admission Type

Used to compare Emergency, Urgent, Elective, and other admission categories.

### Department Name

Used to analyze patient movement and admissions within a selected department.

### Age Group

Used to compare patient flow across different age categories.

### Gender

Used to analyze patient movement based on gender.

### Blood Group

Used to understand patient distribution by blood group.

### Admission Status

Used to filter patients based on their admission or discharge status.

## KPI Cards

### Total Admissions

Shows the total number of admissions during the selected period.

### Average Length of Stay

Shows the average number of days patients stay in the hospital.

### Patients Transferred

Shows the number of patients transferred between departments or wards.

### Transfer Rate

Measures the percentage of patients who were transferred.

### Average Patient Age

Shows the average age of patients included in the selected filters.

### Total Discharges

Shows the total number of discharged patients.

## Charts

### Patient Discharges Trend

A line chart is used to analyze discharge patterns over time.

### Age Group Distribution

A donut chart is used to show the proportion of patients across age groups.

### Patient Transfers by Department

A bar chart is used to identify departments with higher patient transfer activity.

### Admission Type Distribution

A donut or pie chart is used to compare different admission types.

### Length of Stay Distribution

A histogram or bar chart is used to understand how long patients stay in the hospital.

## Dashboard Insight

This dashboard helps management understand patient movement, monitor discharges and transfers, identify departments with high patient flow, and improve patient-flow management.

# 3. Department Analysis Dashboard

## Purpose

The Department Analysis Dashboard compares the performance of different hospital departments. It helps management evaluate patient volume, revenue, length of stay, staff utilization, test results, and readmission patterns.

## Filters

### Department Name

Used to analyze the performance of a selected department.

### Department Type

Used to compare departments based on their department category.

### Admission Date

Used to analyze department performance for a selected date range.

### Age Group

Used to compare department performance across different age groups.

### Gender

Used to analyze department-wise patient distribution by gender.

### Admission Type

Used to compare department performance based on admission categories.

## KPI Cards

### Total Patients

Shows the total number of patients handled by the selected department or departments.

### Total Revenue

Shows the total revenue generated by the selected department.

### Average Length of Stay

Shows the average patient stay for each department.

### Staff Utilization Rate

Shows how effectively staff resources are being utilized.

### Readmission Rate

Measures the percentage of patients readmitted within the selected department.

### Department Efficiency Score

Provides an overall comparison of department operational performance.

## Charts

### Patient Volume by Department

A bar chart is used to compare the number of patients handled by each department.

### Revenue by Department

A bar chart is used to identify departments generating higher revenue.

### Average Length of Stay by Department

A bar chart is used to compare patient stay duration across departments.

### Test Results by Department

A chart is used to analyze test-result distribution across departments.

### Staff Utilization by Department

A bar or pie chart is used to compare staff utilization between departments.

### Readmission Rate by Department

A donut or bar chart is used to identify departments with higher readmission rates.

### Department-wise Performance Comparison

A combined chart or comparison chart is used to compare department performance using multiple operational measures.

## Dashboard Insight

This dashboard helps management identify high-performing departments, compare revenue and patient volume, monitor staff utilization, and identify departments requiring operational improvement.

# 4. Resource Utilization Dashboard

## Purpose

The Resource Utilization Dashboard analyzes the utilization of hospital resources such as beds, staff, equipment, and ICU capacity.

## Filters

### Department Name

Used to analyze resource utilization for a selected department.

### Ward Type

Used to compare resource usage across different ward types.

### Ward Name

Used to analyze bed capacity and utilization for a selected ward.

### Admission Date

Used to analyze resource utilization for a selected time period.

### Bed Status

Used to filter beds based on their current status.

### Equipment Status

Used to analyze equipment based on availability, working condition, or maintenance status.

## KPI Cards

### Resource Utilization Score

Shows the overall utilization level of hospital resources.

### Bed Utilization Rate

Shows the percentage of hospital beds being used.

### Staff Utilization Rate

Shows how effectively hospital staff resources are utilized.

### Available Beds

Shows the number of beds currently available.

### Equipment Utilization Rate

Shows how effectively hospital equipment is being used.

### ICU Capacity

Shows the available and utilized ICU capacity.

## Charts

### Available Beds by Department

A bar chart is used to compare available beds across departments.

### Bed Utilization Rate by Ward Type

A donut or bar chart is used to compare bed utilization across ward types.

### Staff Utilization by Department

A bar or pie chart is used to analyze staff allocation and utilization.

### Equipment Usage

A bar chart is used to compare equipment usage across departments or equipment categories.

### Equipment Status Distribution

A donut chart is used to display working, maintenance, and unavailable equipment.

### ICU Capacity Analysis

A chart is used to compare ICU capacity, occupied beds, and available beds.

### Department-wise Resource Utilization

A bar or treemap chart is used to compare overall resource utilization across departments.

## Dashboard Insight

This dashboard helps management monitor bed availability, staff utilization, equipment usage, and ICU capacity. It supports better resource allocation and operational planning.

# Dashboard Interaction

The dashboards are interactive. Users can select filters to update the KPI cards and charts according to their requirements.

### Filter Interaction

When a filter is selected:

- KPI values are updated.
- Charts display only the selected data.
- Department-wise comparisons change based on the selected filters.
- Revenue, admissions, discharges, and utilization values are recalculated.

### Navigation Interaction

Users can move between dashboards using the navigation buttons:

- Hospital Overview
- Patient Flow
- Department Analysis
- Resource Utilization

# Analytical Story

The dashboard suite follows a complete healthcare operational analysis flow:

Patients → Departments → Resources → Overall Hospital Performance

The dashboards help hospital management:

- Monitor admission and discharge trends.
- Understand patient movement and transfer patterns.
- Compare department-wise performance.
- Analyze revenue and readmission rates.
- Monitor bed and staff utilization.
- Evaluate equipment usage and status.
- Identify areas requiring operational attention.

---

# Important Note

This dashboard is designed for hospital operational monitoring and data analysis. It is not intended for clinical diagnosis, treatment recommendations, or medical decision-making.
